Crab Crunch

Home

Description

What a great recipe! Made it as written, with a couple minor changes: I only had a few croutons left, so I toasted a couple everything bagels and cut them up... plus I add a little Old Bay....it turned out great!!

Ingredients

  • ¼ cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 tablespoon dried minced onion
  • 8 ounces shredded Cheddar cheese
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 325 degrees F (165 degrees C). Lightly grease a medium baking dish.
  2. In large bowl, mix the eggs, milk, croutons, cheese, onion, and parsley. Stir in the crabmeat.
  3. Bake 1 hour in the preheated oven, or until a knife inserted into center of the casserole comes out clean. Serve immediately.
